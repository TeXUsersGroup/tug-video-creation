The presentation explores the operational challenges faced by the publishing industry in managing the end-to-end workflow from manuscript submission to final content delivery across multiple platforms. With the increasing volume and complexity of scholarly and technical content— much of which is authored using LaTeX—publishers worldwide must navigate a complex ecosystem involving diverse stakeholders, evolving technologies, and varied content formats.

Key challenges include integrating LaTeX manuscripts into XML-first and multi-format workflows, accurately converting complex mathematical notations, tables, and figures, and ensuring high-quality typesetting and formatting for print and digital outputs (PDF, HTML, ePub). The presentation highlights operational hurdles such as coordinating peer review, copyediting, and production processes while maintaining the fidelity of original LaTeX source files and meeting tight publication timelines.

A critical and growing area of concern is accessibility. Publishers must ensure that all content complies with accessibility standards such as WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) to serve readers with disabilities. This includes making complex scientific content—including equations, figures, and tables—navigable and interpretable by screen readers and other assistive technologies. Converting LaTeX-based technical manuscripts into accessible formats poses unique challenges, requiring specialized tagging, alternative text descriptions, and semantic markup. Balancing accessibility compliance with preserving the scientific accuracy and visual integrity of content is an ongoing operational challenge for publishers and their suppliers. -1

Additionally, the presentation addresses supply chain and print production logistics, metadata management, rights and licensing complexities, and distribution across global digital platforms. Real-world examples, including the surge in scientific publishing during global events like the COVID-19 pandemic, illustrate how publishers balance quality, speed, accessibility, and market demands.

Attendees will gain insights into best practices and emerging strategies for workflow optimization, automation of LaTeX processing, and effective collaboration between authors, suppliers, and publishers. This knowledge is essential for LaTeX authors and service providers seeking to improve efficiency, quality, and accessibility within the fast-evolving global publishing landscape.
