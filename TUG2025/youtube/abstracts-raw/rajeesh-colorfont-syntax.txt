{An OpenType font with built-in \TeX\ syntax highlighting}
{Syntax highlighting in text editors, \HTML\ pages or typeset documents
require specialized `rules' and addons (such as JavaScript libraries).
OpenType gave programmability and colour to fonts, which could be
leveraged\slash abused to create a font with built-in syntax
highlighting for specific purposes\slash languages. Based on prior
art, we present the development of a webfont that can highlight the
syntax of \TeX{} documents, on browser platforms that support
\acro{COLR}v1 OpenType feature. \preprint}
