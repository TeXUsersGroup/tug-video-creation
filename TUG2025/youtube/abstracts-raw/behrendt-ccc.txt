{Doris Behrendt}
{The \LaTeX\ Village at the\regularorprogramstring{\\}{} Chaos Communication Camp \Dash\programnl how it started, how it continues}
{The Chaos Computer Club (\acro{CCC}) is a German \enquote{Verein} (a
kind of \acro{NGO}) that has been active since 1981. Among other things,
it has organized a hacker congress every year since 1984
(\tbsurl{events.ccc.de/en/congress/}).
In~addition, a camp takes
place every four years since 1999. It features talks, workshops,
\hbox{parties}, and various community-grown projects that are usually organized
into so-called villages (\tbsurl{events.ccc.de/camp/}). Similar
hacker camps also exist in the Netherlands, the \acro{UK} and, as far as
I've heard, also in India.

A \LaTeX\ Village has been held three times so far: in 2017
(Netherlands), 2019, and 2023 (Germany). In my talk, I'll share some
behind-the-scenes insights and report on how, under my direction as a
board member, \DANTE\ e.V. successfully brought these villages to life.

Reports in German about the camps of 2017 and 2019 can be found in
\DANTE's journal \textit{Die~\TeX nische Kom\"odie}: \enquote{Still
hacking anyway\,--\,vom Hacker-Festival in Zeewolde} in issue
2018/4 and \enquote{CCCamp19} in issue 2020/3,
available at
\tbhref{https://dante.de/dtk/bisher-erschienen/}
       {\tt www.dante.de/dtk/bisher-\|erschienen/}. The
report on 2023 is still in progress \dots\,the camp is quite rightly
named \enquote{\textit{Chaos} Communication Camp}. I'll also talk about
this in an informal way and show some pictures.}
