{Doris Behrendt, Bernd Busse}
{How we use \AllTeX\ in the CrypTool project}
{The CrypTool project with its long history uses \LaTeX\ 
in two different scenarios: First, we use \KaTeX\ for maths on our
CrypTool online webpage. Second, we used \LuaLaTeX\ for building the
recently re-edited CrypTool book (in German [1] and English
[2]).

In this talk we limit
ourselves to the topic of mathematical typesetting for
interactive web applications in the browser with \KaTeX.
