{Erik Nijenhuis}
{Modular document engineering with \xdp: From templates to intertwined \PDF{}s}
{In this talk, I will present Xerdi's Documentation Proj\-ect (\xdp)
\Dash a modular framework for managing \LaTeX-based document
production at scale. \xdp\ introduces structured components such as
\textit{Executable Document Templates} (\acro{EDT}s), \textit{Document
Portfolios}, \textit{Workspaces}, and \textit{Data Definition
Repositories}, which together enable a clean separation of template
logic, content, and configuration.

Using this architecture, we'll explore how \LaTeX\ users can
organize their projects using native features like \texttt{TEXMFHOME}
and \texttt{TEXMFDOTDIR}, making it easy to reuse and extend document
workflows across teams or clients.

As a practical demonstration, I'll show how to generate
intertwined documents using \texttt{bib2gls}, \texttt{attachfile2} and
\texttt{hyperref}. This technique can be immediately applied in use
cases such as invoices, reports, or academic materials, and is
accessible to both advanced users and document authors with minimal
coding experience.

Participants will leave with practical tools and insights to better
structure their \LaTeX\ projects\Dash and extend them on their own.
