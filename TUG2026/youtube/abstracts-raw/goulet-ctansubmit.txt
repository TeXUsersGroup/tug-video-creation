{How to make a good first impression: The~\CTAN~guide}
{A new package hits \CTAN\ almost every two days, on average. Very few are
accepted on the first upload. The reasons vary, but they are almost
always addressed in the upload instructions and recommendations. Tune in
if you don't want to \acro{RTFM}: I'll guide you through the most
frequent pitfalls, how to avoid them, and also how to make updates that
live up to your good first impression.
}
