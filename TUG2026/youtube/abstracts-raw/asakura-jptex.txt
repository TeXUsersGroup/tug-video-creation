{Takuto Asakura}
{Japanese \TeX\ in 2026: An outside-in perspective}
{This talk offers a snapshot of the Japanese \TeX\ ecosystem in 2026,
intended for an international audience. It covers what makes Japanese
typesetting structurally different from Western typesetting, the two
practical workflows in common use today (p\TeX\ variants and
Lua\TeX-ja), and current challenges around technical debt, kernel-level
evolution, and the tentative np\TeX\ concept.

The motivation is to help bridge a persistent information gap. The
recent \TUB\ overview by Berry, Chernoff, and Hef{}feron (2025,
\url{https://tug.org/TUGboat/\\tb46-3/tb144berry-engines-formats.pdf}),
for example, notes that the (e)(u)p\TeX\ engine ``remains very popular
in the Japanese community, but is rarely used by others since
English-language documentation is limited.'' This talk attempts to fill
in some of that blank space, offered from the perspective of an observer
of both the local Japanese and the global \TeX\ communities.
}
