{Clemens Lode}
{Real-time Lua\TeX: Recompiling large documents in 1ms}
{When Typst emerged as a modern alternative to \LaTeX, offering real-time 
preview, simpler syntax, and browser-based editing, it raised an 
uncomfortable question for \LaTeX-based publishing: why should authors 
endure compile cycles and complex toolchains when a modern alternative 
exists?

Rather than switching, I set out to bring the same experience to \LuaTeX
\ itself. The result is \texttt{texlode}, a browser-based editor that
typesets books with \LuaTeX\ in real time. No compile button, no local
installation, no \LaTeX\ knowledge required, but full \texttt{microtype}
experience at 1ms compile speed, beating Typst in all benchmarks.

The project grew out of a decade of \LaTeX\ publishing services working 
with dozens of clients, and my book \titleref{How to Publish Your Book with 
\LaTeX}, which documents the complete self-publishing workflow using the 
\acro{LODE} template. \texttt{texlode} turns that workflow into an
interactive editor:
 authors import a Word, Markdown, or \LaTeX\ manuscript;
 see it typeset live;
 generate covers; and
 publish to print-on-demand and e-book platforms from a single source.

\noindent
This talk demonstrates the editor and discusses what it took to make 
\LuaTeX\ competitive with Typst as a real-time authoring experience while 
preserving the power of the \TeX\ ecosystem.
