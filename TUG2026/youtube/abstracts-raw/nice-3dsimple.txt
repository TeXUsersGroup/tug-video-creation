{Jasper Nice}
{Simple \acro{3D} illustrations in the \TeX\ ecosystem}
{The talk will examine an existing framework for reasoning about 3D
mathematical illustrations which are composed of triangles and line
segments, as well as frame a pervasive issue through the lens of this
framework. Specifically, we will investigate the principles of occlusion
and partitioning, and we will observe the inadequacies of current
tessellation theory.
}
