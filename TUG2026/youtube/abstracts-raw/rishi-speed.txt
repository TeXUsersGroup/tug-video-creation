{\TeX\ and \XML-based authoring tools}
{Speed plays a crucial role in journal publication. Publishers
are competing to shorten production time, accelerate the publication
process, and reduce costs. \TeX\ has become increasingly popular, with
more authors using it to prepare their manuscripts. Since \XML\ is one
of the primary deliverables, converting complex \TeX\ manuscripts, often
containing extensive mathematical content, into \XML\slash\MathML\
within a short time frame remains a significant challenge. Typesetters
who rely predominantly on \TeX\ now face difficulties: publishers demand
\XML\slash\MathML\ outputs within hours, while authors expect their
documents to be typeset in \TeX\ with the highest precision and quality.
This article examines these challenges in detail and attempts to find a
solution.}
