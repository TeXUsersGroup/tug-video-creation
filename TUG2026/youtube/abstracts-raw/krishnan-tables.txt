{The pursuit of professional and accessible tables in \LaTeX}
{In academic publishing, typesetting tables remains a significant 
challenge, requiring a delicate balance between rigorous publisher 
styling and modern accessibility mandates. The Comprehensive \TeX\ 
Archive Network (\CTAN) provides numerous options, such as booktabs, 
tabularx, tabulary, longtable, tabularray, etc. Finding a single 
package among these that offers features like column and cell 
merging, cell and row colors, page breaking, caption styling, 
footnotes remains a persistent challenge. In this talk, we will 
compare the features of prominent packages to determine which 
tools are most effective for creating professional-grade 
tables that remain fully accessible to all readers.}
