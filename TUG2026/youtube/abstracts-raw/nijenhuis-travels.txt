{Erik Nijenhuis}
{Meeting \TeX\ friends: Adventures across \acro{LUG}s}
{Over the past years, I have had the opportunity to visit several \TeX-related
conferences and meetings, including \acro{TUG} 2024/2025, Gu\acro{IT} 2025,
\acro{DANTE} 2026, Bacho\TeX\slash Euro\TeX\ 2026, and the \acro{NTG
AGM} 2025/2026.

This talk is a personal and informal reflection on those travels: the
people met along the way, the cultural differences between \TeX\ user
groups, the hallway discussions, and the surprisingly international
nature of the \TeX\ community. From technical conversations to local
traditions and late-night discussions, the presentation offers a glimpse
into the social side of the \TeX\ ecosystem that often remains invisible
from mailing lists and repositories.
