{Rajeesh KV, Radhakrishnan CV}
{Digital archiving of Malayalam content \acro{II}}
{This presentation is a sequel to the one with the same name made during
\acro{TUG} 2025. The previous talk described an archival technique using
\TeX, but without the help of \TeX4ht owing to its inability to process
\tbUTF-8 content (then). Now that \TeX4ht can digest \tbUTF-8 content,
we have revised the workflow making use of the newer capabilities that
include math processing.

In the revised workflow, we employ \TeX4ht to process the \TeX{} sources
to generate an enriched \acro{HTML} output which in turn is used as
the source to create \acro{XML} output. We adopted this deviation from the
traditional procedure (\TeX{}$\longrightarrow$ \acro{XML} $\longrightarrow$
\acro{HTML}) due to the practical consideration of using a volunteer
workforce with lesser expertise in markup formats, who find it difficult to
fix parser-generated errors while validating the \acro{XML} output by editing
the \TeX{} sources.

The current process splits the workload between the two categories of
average volunteers and (scarcely available) expert ones, the latter being
engaged solely for \XML\ production. The talk provides more information
about the aforementioned workflow and related technologies.}
