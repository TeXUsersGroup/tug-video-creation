{Tristan Miller}
{Preparing \acro{SSHRC} Insight Grant applications in {\LaTeX} with~\programrlap{\texttt{sshrc-insight}}}
{Canada's Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council (\acro{SSHRC}), like 
many other funding agencies, provides an online submission portal that
requires proposals to be structured into a multitude of plain-text form
fields and \PDF\ attachments. Such workflows make it challenging for
grant applicants to maintain the consistency of formatting and
cross-referencing across multiple submission components, particularly
when using a conventional word processor.

This presentation introduces \texttt{sshrc-insight}, a \LaTeX\ class for
preparing applications to \acro{SSHRC}'s Insight Grants program. Besides
formatting the proposal according to \acro{SSHRC}'s typographical
specifications, the class allows authors to easily switch between
producing multiple \PDF{}s, each corresponding to an individual portal
upload, or a single, unified \PDF\ that is more convenient to peruse
before submission. Additional features include stable citation numbering
across compilation modes, character counts for text destined for
web-form fields, and bi\-lingual English/French support.

Beyond providing an overview of the class and its intended workflow,
this presentation examines the technical challenges encountered during
its design and implementation. It thus aims not only to introduce a
practical tool for grant writers, but also to provide guidance to
developers designing classes for similar fragmented, portal-driven
document workflows.
}
