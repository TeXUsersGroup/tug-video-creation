{Erik Nijenhuis}
{Experimental lists and titling for \PDFUA-2 compliance}
{As the author of the \texttt{regulatory} package, I initially overlooked
accessibility concerns. I later discovered that common dependencies such
as \texttt{enumitem} and \texttt{titlesec} prevent proper structural
tagging, making compliance with \PDFUA-2\Dash and thus user-accessible
documents\Dash unattainable.

This talk explores recent developments from the \LaTeX\ Project, including
\LaTeX-lab components and kernel-level mechanisms, to address these
limitations. Although some features are still experimental, they already
enable structure-aware lists and sectioning.

Once these approaches mature, they will provide a viable path to making
packages like \texttt{regulatory} fully accessible.
