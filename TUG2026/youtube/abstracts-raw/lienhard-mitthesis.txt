{John Lienhard}
{Modernizing \acro{MIT}'s thesis template: \texttt{mitthesis.cls}}
{A few years ago, I began an update to \acro{MIT}'s \LaTeX\ template for theses
and dissertations. The previous template had been written in \LaTeX~2.09
in the 1980s and patched for \LaTeXe\ by various students during the
1990s. My work quickly turned into a complete rewrite. My goals were to
use current \LaTeX\ methods and to produce tagged \PDF, while still
supporting users who have minimal knowledge of \LaTeX\ and typesetting. In
this talk, I will discuss some of the considerations that shaped the
current template.
}
