{River Temple}
{From neurons to \acro{NASA}: A brief tour of the brain, font psychology, and implications in spaceflight missions}
{Understanding how the brain reads and interprets visual cues sets the
stage for exploring why typography matters, especially in environments
where clarity and speed are of utmost importance. This presentation
offers a brief tour of core neural systems involved in attention,
pattern recognition, and reading, into the practical psychology of
typefaces, and the implications therein which should be considered for
those who are designing documents for end users in mind.

In the specific instance offered in this presentation, astronauts rely
on dense checklists, instrument panels, and digital readouts, often
under stress, fatigue, or other physiologically demanding factors of the
spaceflight experience. Small typographic choices such as font weight,
x-height, line spacing, and symbol clarity can influence how quickly and
accurately information is processed in micro\-gravity or emergency
conditions.

This presentation will lay a framework for the shared ground between
neuroscience, typography, and aerospace design. The aim is to highlight
promising intersections and open the door for future presentations that
explore each area in greater depth.}
