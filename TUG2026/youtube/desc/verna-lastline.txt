The glue model of TeX is a powerful and elegant abstraction allowing for all sorts of typographic applications. One such application is the typesetting of a paragraph's last line, which requires special treatment. By adding an infinitely stretchable glue at the end of a paragraph, the need for special-casing the final line is avoided, albeit at the cost of sub-optimal typesetting.

In conscientious typography, optimally typesetting the last line of a paragraph turns out to be a non-trivial problem, especially if one wants to take into account such aesthetic concerns as runt or full-out lines. In this talk, we present an algorithm for optimal last line adjustment, introducing the concept of “quantic boundaries”: potential line breaks, the exact state of which remains unknown until an actual paragraph solution is observed.
