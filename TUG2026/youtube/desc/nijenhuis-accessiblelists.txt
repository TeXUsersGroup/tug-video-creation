As the author of the regulatory package, I initially overlooked accessibility concerns. I later discovered that common dependencies such as enumitem and titlesec prevent proper structural tagging, making compliance with PDF/UA-2—and thus user-accessible documents—unattainable.

This talk explores recent developments from the LaTeX Project, including LaTeX-lab components and kernel-level mechanisms, to address these limitations. Although some features are still experimental, they already enable structure-aware lists and sectioning.

Once these approaches mature, they will provide a viable path to making packages like regulatory fully accessible.
